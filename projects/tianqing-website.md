## Problem

A professional-services website needs clear service information, consistent business data, and content that avoids unsupported promises.

## My implementation work

Implementation of a client website with reusable content structures, responsive pages, technical SEO, and source-aware content governance.

## Technical scope

- Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- Structured service and announcement data feeding page generation and metadata.
- JSON-LD, sitemap, robots configuration, and content-audit tooling.
- Shared business information and documented rules for service claims.

## Evidence and current scope

[Public client website](https://www.tqcpb.com). The visible pages demonstrate the website and content organization. Search ranking, conversion gains, and regulatory approval are not claimed as measured outcomes.

## Relationship to other work

A client website project. This public case omits contact submissions, private client material, and operational configuration.

## Engineering perspective

Reusable content structures reduce inconsistency across pages. For a regulated-service context, accurate wording and source traceability are part of engineering quality.
