# FocusOYL-MathMorph — Model Editing Research

**Author:** Daniel Tang  
**Project type:** Experimental AI/model-evaluation research  
**Public repository:** [FocusOYL-MathMorph](https://github.com/yo20ywork-max/FocusOYL-MathMorph)

FocusOYL-MathMorph explores training-free GGUF spectral editing, mathematical methods, controlled experiments, and negative results.

The [project repository](https://github.com/yo20ywork-max/FocusOYL-MathMorph) is the primary location for its research documentation, implementation, evidence, and current publication status. Consult that repository for the scope of available material and its stated results.

## Relationship to other projects

FocusOYL-MathMorph is a separate research project from the [FocusOYL translator and agent](focusoyl.md) and [Focus Oyl Reminders](focus-oyl-reminders.md). The shared naming does not make their implementation or validation results interchangeable.
