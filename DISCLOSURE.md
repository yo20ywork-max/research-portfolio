# Public Disclosure Scope

This repository contains newly written portfolio documentation. It does not reproduce private source histories or publish internal operating records.

## Included

- Project purpose and the practical problem addressed.
- General implementation areas and technology choices.
- Public product URLs and already-public source repositories.
- Development relationships and explicit implementation limitations.
- Author attribution and links to relevant third-party notices.

## Excluded

- Credentials, session material, signing material, and private configuration.
- Customer or employee records, conversations, production exports, and internal logs.
- Unpublished patent material, proprietary algorithms, internal prompts, and confidential operating procedures.
- Private administration screens, infrastructure identifiers, and deployment secrets.

Source-repository visibility is separate from portfolio visibility. A public case study is available for every listed repository, including those whose operational source remains private. Public descriptions do not grant rights to company assets, client content, trademarks, model weights, or other third-party materials.

The case studies do not claim patent grants, formal security certifications, user counts, revenue, or performance improvements that have not been established.
